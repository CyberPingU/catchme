CatchMe is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted messaging application with proximity discovery capabilities.

Discover and chat with nearby people without ever sharing your identity, phone number, or exact location with a central server.

Features:
- End-to-end encrypted messaging (ECDH X25519 + AES-GCM)
- Proximity discovery via GPS and WebSocket server relay
- 100% FOSS flavor with UnifiedPush support (no Google Play Services needed)
- No account, no phone number, no tracking
- Self-hostable backend support
